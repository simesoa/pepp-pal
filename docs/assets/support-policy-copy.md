## What Penn Pal Is

Penn Pal is a 1-on-1 anonymous messaging app for college students. We take your privacy seriously — not because a lawyer told us to, but because the whole app depends on it.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what we don't do with it.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information you provide

**Email address.**
You provide a .edu email address when you create your account. We use it to authenticate you and send you password reset emails if you request one. We do not display your email to other users.

**Password.**
Your password is hashed by Supabase Auth before storage. We never have access to your plaintext password.

**Graduation year.**
You select your graduation year during signup. We use it to match you with someone in the same graduating class.

**Messages.**
The messages you send in Penn Pal are stored in our database to make the conversation available when you open the app. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted — they are stored and accessible to our infrastructure for moderation purposes if a conversation is reported.

**Optional prompt.**
When you sign up, you may optionally share what you're going through. This is used to inform matching and is not shown to your Penn Pal.

### Information we collect automatically

**Analytics events.**
We log anonymized usage events (for example: "a user completed signup", "a conversation started") to understand how the app is used and to improve it. These events do not include message content, email addresses, or any personally identifying information.

---

## How We Use Your Information

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Email address | Authentication, password reset |
| Graduation year | Matching |
| Messages | Delivering the chat experience; moderation of reported conversations |
| Analytics events | Aggregate product improvement — no individual profiling |

---

## What We Don't Do

- We do not sell your data to anyone.
- We do not show you ads.
- We do not share your information with third parties, except as required by law.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.
- We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.

---

## Moderation

Penn Pal moderates reported conversations. If you report a conversation, our team will review the messages in that conversation. We do not proactively read messages or conduct mass surveillance of conversations.

Accounts that violate our Community Guidelines may be suspended.

---

## Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, your login credentials and account record are permanently removed. Your messages are anonymized — they are no longer linked to your account, but they remain in the conversation history so your Penn Pal's chat is preserved.

If you'd like us to remove your anonymized messages as well, email us at support@pennpal.app and we'll do so manually.

---

## Your Rights

You can:
- Access the information we hold about you by emailing support@pennpal.app
- Request deletion of your account in-app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing us
- Request correction of your account email by emailing us

If you are in the European Economic Area or UK, you have additional rights under GDPR. Contact us at support@pennpal.app.

---

## Security

We use Supabase for authentication and database storage. Supabase provides encryption at rest and in transit. Row-level security policies ensure users can only read their own data and the data they're permitted to access.

No security measure is perfect. If you believe you've found a security issue, please email support@pennpal.app.

---

## Children

Penn Pal requires a .edu email address and is intended for college students (generally 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at support@pennpal.app.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We'll notify users of material changes through the app or by email. The "last updated" date at the top of this document reflects when changes were made. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

---

## Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@pennpal.app.

# 3. Community Guidelines

---

## Penn Pal is a private space. Treat it like one.

You don't know who your Penn Pal is. They don't know who you are. That anonymity is what makes honest conversation possible — but it also means we all have to be deliberate about how we show up here.

These guidelines aren't a long list of rules. They're the spirit of what Penn Pal is trying to be.

---

## Be kind

Your Penn Pal is a real person going through college at the same time as you. They're probably dealing with some of the same things. Talk to them the way you'd want someone to talk to you on a hard day.

---

## Stay anonymous

Don't share your name, phone number, email address, social media handles, or other identifying information. The app will try to block this automatically — but the spirit of the rule matters too. If you're trying to find out who your Penn Pal is, you're using Penn Pal wrong.

If your Penn Pal shares identifying information, don't push for it and don't share your own in return.

---

## Don't send anything you wouldn't want a stranger to read

Because your Penn Pal is, for now, a stranger. Keep it appropriate. No explicit content. No threats. No harassment.

---

## Report what's wrong

Penn Pal has a one-tap report option for a reason. If your conversation goes somewhere unsafe, use it. You can also block and rematch — which ends the conversation immediately and puts you back in the matching pool.

Every report is reviewed by a real person within 24 hours.

---

## What gets you suspended

- Harassment, threats, or bullying
- Sexual content (explicit or pressuring)
- Sharing or trying to extract personal identity information
- Repeated boundary-crossing after your Penn Pal has signaled discomfort
- Using Penn Pal for anything other than honest peer conversation (spam, solicitation, etc.)

Suspended accounts lose access to Penn Pal. You can appeal to support@pennpal.app.

---

## If something comes up in conversation

Penn Pal has crisis resources built in — one tap from inside the app. If your Penn Pal shares something that concerns you, it's okay to let them know that help exists.

988 Lifeline: call or text 988
Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741

Penn Pal is peer connection, not a mental health service. But we take the wellbeing of our users seriously, and we've tried to make it easy to point to real help when it's needed.

---

# 4. Support Page

## We're here.

Penn Pal is a small team. If something's not working, or you have a question, or you just want to say something — we read every email.

**support@pennpal.app**

We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.

---

## Common questions

**I signed up but I haven't been matched yet.**
You'll land on a waiting screen while we look for someone in your graduating class. As more students sign up with the same graduation year, you'll be matched automatically. You don't need to do anything — we'll notify you in the app when a match is found.

**How do I leave a conversation and find a new match?**
Open the chat → tap ⋯ in the top-right corner → tap "Request rematch." You'll leave the conversation and be re-entered into the matching pool.

**How do I report a conversation?**
Open the chat → tap ⋯ → tap "Report conversation." Select a reason. Our team will review it within 24 hours.

**How do I delete my account?**
Settings → Delete account. This is permanent. See our account deletion page for more detail.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password" and we'll send a reset link to your .edu email.

**I was suspended and I think it was a mistake.**
Email us at support@pennpal.app with the subject "Account Suspension Appeal." Include the email address on your account. We'll review and respond.

**I found a bug.**
We'd love to know. Email support@pennpal.app with a description of what happened and what device you're on.

---

## Crisis support

Penn Pal is peer connection — not a mental health service. If you're in crisis, please reach out to real help:

- **988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline:** call or text **988**
- **Crisis Text Line:** text **HOME** to **741741**
- **Emergency:** call **911**

These are also accessible inside the Penn Pal app, one tap from any chat.

## Deleting your Penn Pal account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app. Here's exactly what happens when you do.

---

## How to delete your account

1. Open Penn Pal
2. Tap the ⋯ menu or go to **Settings**
3. Tap **Delete account**
4. Confirm in the prompt

That's it. The deletion happens immediately.

---

## What gets removed

**Your login credentials.** Your account is permanently deleted from our authentication system. You will not be able to sign back in with the same email.

**Your account record.** Your user profile — including your graduation year, match status, and any account flags — is permanently deleted.

---

## What happens to your messages

Your messages are **anonymized**, not deleted.

This is intentional. Your Penn Pal's conversation history is preserved — the messages are still there, but they're no longer linked to your account or identity. From their perspective, the conversation remains intact but the other participant is now anonymous.

If you'd like your messages removed entirely, email us at support@pennpal.app after deleting your account and we'll remove them manually.

---

## What we don't keep

After deletion, we do not retain:
- Your email address (linked to your account)
- Your login credentials
- Your graduation year (linked to your account)
- Your match status or pair relationships

---

## Can I re-register after deleting?

Yes. You can sign up again with the same .edu email address. You'll start fresh — you won't be reconnected with your previous Penn Pal.

---

## I want to delete but I can't access the app

Email us at support@pennpal.app from the .edu address on your account. We'll process the deletion manually and confirm when it's done.
