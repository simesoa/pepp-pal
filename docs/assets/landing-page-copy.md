## How It Works

**Section header:**
> Simple on purpose.

**Three steps:**

**1. Sign up with your .edu email**
Your school email confirms you're a college student. We don't share it or show it to anyone. It's just how we know you belong here.

**2. Get matched by graduation year**
Penn Pal pairs you with one person from your graduating class. You won't know who they are. They won't know who you are. That's the whole idea.

**3. Talk — or don't**
Some pairs talk every day. Some check in once a week. Some sit in silence for a while. There's no pressure. No notifications nudging you. Just one open conversation, whenever you need it.

## Safety Section

**Section header:**
> Safe space isn't just something we say.

**Body:**
Penn Pal is built around a few hard rules:

**No identity sharing.** Penn Pal's filter automatically blocks phone numbers, emails, social handles, and other identifying information. Anonymity is protected, not just requested.

**Report anything.** Every conversation has a one-tap report option. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours.

**Leave anytime.** Not feeling the connection? Request a rematch. You'll both be re-entered into the matching pool, no explanation needed.

**Crisis resources, one tap away.** The 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline and Crisis Text Line are accessible directly inside the app. If something comes up in conversation, help is right there.

**No data sold. Ever.** Penn Pal doesn't sell your data, show you ads, or share your information with third parties. Your email stays private. Your messages stay private.

## FAQ

**Who can use Penn Pal?**
Anyone with a valid .edu email address. Penn Pal is designed for college students, and your school email is how we verify that.

**How does matching work?**
You select your graduation year when you sign up. Penn Pal pairs you with one other person who has the same graduation year. Matching happens automatically — usually within a few seconds if there's someone waiting.

**What if there's no one to match me with?**
You'll land on a waiting screen. As more people sign up with your graduation year, Penn Pal will match you when the right person arrives. We'll let you know in the app.

**Do I stay matched with the same person forever?**
The intention is yes — one person, your whole college journey. But you can leave a conversation and request a new match at any time, no questions asked.

**Can I find out who my Penn Pal is?**
Not through the app. Penn Pal's identity filter actively prevents either of you from sharing names, phone numbers, emails, or social handles. If you both want to reveal yourselves, that's between you — Penn Pal just won't help you do it automatically.

**Is Penn Pal monitored?**
Conversations are private. Reports are reviewed by our team. We do not proactively read messages, but reported conversations are reviewed by a human within 24 hours.

**What happens to my data if I delete my account?**
Your account and login are permanently removed. Your messages are anonymized — your Penn Pal's conversation history is preserved, but your messages are no longer linked to your account.

**Is Penn Pal free?**
Yes, during the pilot. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no ads.
