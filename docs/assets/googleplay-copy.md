## Full Description (4,000 characters max)

```
Penn Pal pairs you with one anonymous person in your graduating class.

No social feed. No profile to maintain. No followers to perform for. Just one quiet, honest connection — someone walking the same path, at the same time, toward the same finish line.

You sign up with your .edu email. Penn Pal matches you by graduation year. Neither of you knows who the other is. You just talk.

The anonymity is the point. It's easier to be honest when there's no name attached. To ask for help without worrying what people think. To say the thing you haven't said out loud yet.

Some pairs talk every day. Some check in once a month. Some sit in silence for a while — and that's fine too.

—

WHAT PENN PAL IS

• A private 1-on-1 space matched by graduation year
• Anonymous — no names, no photos, no profiles
• Built around your .edu email — college students only
• One conversation at a time, for your whole college journey

—

WHAT PENN PAL ISN'T

• Not a social network
• Not a dating app
• Not a group chat
• Not somewhere to perform or accumulate anything

—

BUILT FOR SAFETY

Penn Pal is designed to be a safe space — not just a statement.

You can report any conversation in one tap. Every report is reviewed by our team within 24 hours. You can leave and request a new match at any time. Crisis resources — including the 988 Lifeline and Crisis Text Line — are accessible in one tap from inside the app.

Your anonymity is protected. Penn Pal's identity filter automatically blocks messages that contain phone numbers, emails, social media handles, or other identifying information.

—

Penn Pal is for the moments when you need someone who gets it — not because they know you, but because they're in it too.
```

*(~1,450 characters)*
